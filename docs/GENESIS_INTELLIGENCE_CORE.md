# ICE Genesis Intelligence Core

## Mission

The Genesis Intelligence Core is the central reasoning engine of ICE Genesis.

It does not detect market events.

Instead, it interprets information from every engine and determines the institutional narrative, execution readiness, and trade permission.

# Genesis Intelligence Core

The Intelligence Core combines every engine into one institutional interpretation.

Outputs

- Market Direction
- Institutional Narrative
- Current Objective
- Trade Permission
- Confidence
- Next Required Event

---

# Decision Engine

The Decision Engine is the final gate.

Possible outputs

- BUY
- SELL
- WAIT
- NO TRADE

The Decision Engine never overrides the Institutional Bias Engine.

Lower timeframes refine entries.

Higher timeframes determine direction.

---

# Philosophy

The purpose of ICE Genesis is not to predict the future.

Its purpose is to think like an institutional trader and communicate the current market story in a way that helps the trader make disciplined decisions.
