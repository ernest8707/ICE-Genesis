# ICE Genesis Sniper Entry Model

## Core Principle

Higher timeframes determine direction.

Lower timeframes determine entry.

The two most important entry conditions are:

1. A liquidity sweep
2. A retest into a valid order block

## Bullish Sniper Entry

Required sequence:

- Higher-timeframe bias is bullish
- Sell-side liquidity is swept
- Bullish displacement confirms
- Bullish CHoCH or BOS confirms
- A valid bullish order block forms
- Price retests the bullish order block
- Lower-timeframe confirmation appears
- Decision Engine approves LONG

## Bearish Sniper Entry

Required sequence:

- Higher-timeframe bias is bearish
- Buy-side liquidity is swept
- Bearish displacement confirms
- Bearish CHoCH or BOS confirms
- A valid bearish order block forms
- Price retests the bearish order block
- Lower-timeframe confirmation appears
- Decision Engine approves SHORT

## Mandatory A-Grade Conditions

- Higher-timeframe directional alignment
- Correct-side liquidity sweep
- Valid order block
- Retest into the order block
- Lower-timeframe structure confirmation

## Supporting Conditions

- Strong displacement
- Active FVG
- OTE alignment
- Premium or discount alignment
- Kill-zone timing
- Confirmation candle

## Entry States

- WAITING FOR LIQUIDITY
- WAITING FOR ORDER BLOCK
- WAITING FOR RETEST
- WAITING FOR CONFIRMATION
- LONG READY
- SHORT READY
- COUNTERTREND BLOCKED
- NO TRADE

## Default Scoring Weights

- HTF alignment: 20
- Liquidity sweep: 20
- Order-block retest: 20
- BOS or CHoCH: 15
- Displacement: 10
- FVG alignment: 5
- OTE / premium-discount: 5
- Confirmation candle: 5
